About OmniBrain Quant

An advanced agentic AI orchestration platform designed for Wall Street-grade financial analysis using Multi-Modal RAG.

architecture System Architecture

OmniBrain Quant leverages a state-of-the-art LangGraph Supervisor Orchestrator to seamlessly route analyst queries across specialized autonomous agents.

  • SearchAgent (Self-RAG): Performs dense vector retrieval on high-density financial documents (SEC 10-K, Earnings Transcripts) with an integrated reflection loop to iteratively refine search queries until the perfect context is found.
  • VisionAgent (Multi-Modal): Uses advanced Vision-Language Models (VLMs) like Google Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite to extract tabular data, charts, and diagrams with localized bounding boxes.
  • SQLAgent: Synthesizes Natural Language to SQL, querying structured tabular metrics directly from DuckDB or SQLite for precise quantitative comparisons.
  • Synthesizer & NeMo Guardrails: Merges cross-modal data into highly polished investment memorandums. Enforces absolute deterministic grounding and faithfulness via NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails to prevent hallucinations.
